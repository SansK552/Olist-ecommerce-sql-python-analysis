# End-to-End E-commerce Performance & Customer Insights Analysis of Olist Dataset

This project analyzes Brazilian e-commerce data to derive business insights related to delivery performance, customer behavior, payment trends, product performance and customer satisfaction.

### Business questions

1️⃣ Delivery Performance

What percentage of orders were delivered on or before the estimated delivery date?<br>
What is the average delivery delay (in days)?<br>
Which states experience the highest delivery delays?<br>

2️⃣ Payment Behavior

What is the most frequently used payment type?<br>
Is there a correlation between payment value and number of installments?<br>
Do higher installment payments lead to higher order values?<br>

3️⃣ Customer Satisfaction

What is the average review score by state and city?<br>
Does delivery delay impact review score?<br>
How long does it take (in days) for reviews to be answered?<br>

4️⃣ Revenue & Product Analysis

Which product categories generate the highest revenue?<br>
Which product categories have the lowest review scores?<br>
Do heavier/larger products lead to longer delivery times?<br>

### SQL Analysis

SQL was used to transform raw transactional data into structured business metrics by:<br>
  • Calculating the percentage of orders delivered on or before the estimated date.<br>
  • Measuring average delivery delay in days using actual vs estimated delivery timelines.<br>
  • Identifying states with the highest late delivery percentages.<br>
  • Computing payment method usage share to determine dominant transaction modes.<br>
  • Evaluating whether higher installment counts are associated with higher order values.<br>
  • Aggregating review scores by state and city to assess regional customer satisfaction.<br>
  • Measuring average review response time in days.<br>
  • Calculating total revenue generated by each product category.<br>
  • Identifying product categories with lower review scores but significant revenue impact.<br>
  • Filtering only meaningful data (e.g., delivered orders, valid timestamps).<br>
  • Combining multiple datasets (orders, customers, reviews, products, payments) to create unified analytical views.<br>
  • Ranking states and product categories based on delay, revenue, and satisfaction metrics.

The queries were structured to support decision-making across logistics, payments, customer experience and product strategy.

### Key Insights

1️⃣ Delivery Performance

  • 91.89% of orders were delivered on or before the estimated date.<br>
  • Delivery delay distribution is slightly left-skewed (many early deliveries).<br>
  • Significant outliers exist (some orders delayed by 100+ days).<br>
  • States like AL and MA show relatively higher late delivery percentages compared to national average.<br>

2️⃣ Payment Behavior

  • Credit Card dominates (73.92%) of total payment methods.<br>
  • Boleto accounts for ~19%.<br>
  • Clear upward trend: higher installment count → higher average order value.<br>
  • Positive correlation between payment value and number of installments.<br>

3️⃣ Customer Satisfaction

  • Average review scores across states mostly range between 3.7 – 4.4.<br>
  • Higher delivery delays are associated with lower review scores.<br>
  • Review response time averages around 2–3 days.<br>
  • Faster responses slightly correlate with better ratings.<br>

4️⃣ Product & Revenue Analysis

  • Top revenue categories include beleza_saude, relogios_presentes, cama_mesa_banho.<br>
  • Some high-revenue categories have relatively lower review scores.<br>
  • Weak-to-moderate relationship between product weight and delivery time.<br>
  • Revenue does not strongly correlate with review score — high revenue does not always mean high satisfaction.<br>

### Business Recommendations 

1️⃣ Logistics Optimization

  • Investigate extreme delay outliers (100+ day delays).<br>
  • Improve delivery efficiency in high-delay states.<br>
  • Maintain strong performance (91.89% on-time is a competitive advantage).<br>

2️⃣ Payment Strategy

  • Promote installment plans for higher-ticket products.<br>
  • Offer targeted installment promotions in high-value categories.<br>

3️⃣ Customer Satisfaction Improvement

  • Prioritize faster resolution for low-rated reviews.<br>
  • Focus on categories with high revenue but lower average ratings.<br>
  • Implement proactive communication for delayed shipments.<br>

4️⃣ Product & Operations

  • Optimize shipping for heavier products.<br>
  • Conduct quality checks in lower-rated categories.<br>
  • Balance revenue growth with customer satisfaction metrics.<br>
